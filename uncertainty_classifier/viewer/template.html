Error Viewer

Overall Metrics by Baseline

Mean over the filtered runs of each baseline, all on a 0–1 scale. Success: share of runs that met the success criteria. Completion: share of goal propositions met. SPL_act and TC-SPL_act weight success and completion by Aref / max(A, Aref) (definitions under Task Metrics below). Click a bar to list that baseline's runs.

Failure Category Prevalence by Baseline

Share of all of each baseline's runs with each failure category (failure categories are only checked on failed runs). A run can have several. Click a bar to list those runs.

Inefficiency Category Prevalence by Baseline

Share of each baseline's runs (successful or not) with each inefficiency category (Unnecessary Action includes Repeated Action unless shown separately). Click a bar to list those runs.

Error Category Prevalence by Baseline and Condition

Share of runs with each category, split by the chosen dimension. Failures are a share of failed runs in that column; inefficiencies a share of all runs. Unnecessary Action includes Repeated Action unless shown separately. Click a cell to list those runs.

Task Metrics by Baseline and Condition

Mean over runs, by baseline and the same dimension. Success: share of runs that met the task's success criteria. Completion: share of goal propositions met (timeouts keep partial completion). SPL_act = S · Aref / max(A, Aref) and TC-SPL_act = C · Aref / max(A, Aref), where A is the run's recorded skill attempts (failures included, Rearrange counted as 4, Done/ReportAbsence excluded) and Aref the human ground-truth action count of the variant.

Judge-Attributed Causes by Module and Baseline

The LLM judge's attributions by module, by baseline. Tick modules to compare their error types instead, or show all error types. Each attribution counts once, under the module and error type the judge gave it; that includes attributions for errors no rule flagged ("extra", counted in the hover). An observed error can get several attributions. Click a bar or label to list those runs.

Observed Error Category vs. Judge-Attributed Cause

Attributions for each observed-error label (column) by the error type the judge gave (row). Rows follow the modules ticked above. "Extra" holds errors no rule flagged. Click a cell to list those runs.
