Average time per task

Elapsed time is the measured or recovered recording session interval, including action processing, rendering, encoding, and polling; HTTP-derived intervals can include human pauses. It excludes scene loading and final saving. Active skill time is measured inside the simulator worker and includes skill execution, rendering, and encoding; it excludes loading, final saving, and gaps between commands. Video time is playback length. New single-sandbox measurements are labeled separately from historical timings collected under different machine loads. Missing or replaced recordings have no elapsed value and are excluded from averages. Coverage shows timed / saved recordings. All averages follow the filters; the overall average weights each available recording equally.
